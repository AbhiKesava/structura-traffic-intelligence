PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Challenge of Traffic Congestion

"Traffic congestion makes it difficult for people to know how long a journey will take. Unexpected slowdowns can lead to wasted time and fuel, missed appointments, and delays for public transport or emergency services. People often have to make travel decisions without knowing how busy a road is likely to be."

"This project explores how historical and available traffic data can be used to estimate congestion on selected road segments for a future time. The system presents those estimates clearly so users can understand current conditions, compare roads or departure times, and make more informed travel plans."

Congestion Severity Classification Standards
